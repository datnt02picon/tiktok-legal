Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

1. Overview

This application (the "App") is a personal tool used by its owner to publish the owner's own short videos to the owner's own social media accounts through official APIs.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or share personal information from other users. To publish videos, the App uses the owner's own account credentials (OAuth access and refresh tokens), which are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared with any third party.

3. How We Use Information

Access tokens are used solely to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own account via the official TikTok Content Posting API. The App does not access, read, or collect data from any other account.

4. Data Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. No analytics or tracking of end users is performed.

5. Data Storage and Security

Credentials are kept only on the owner's local device and are not transmitted anywhere except to the official TikTok API endpoints required to publish videos.

6. Third-Party Services

The App interacts with TikTok's official APIs. Use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
